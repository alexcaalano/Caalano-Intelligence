**DRAFT for legal review. Not yet in use.** Template to be attached to every customer subscription before the first customer outside Caalano Digital connects an account (SAAS-DESIGN.md, section 11). Square brackets mark facts and choices to confirm.

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between **Caalano Digital Pty Ltd** (ABN 31 670 857 397) ("Caalano Digital", "we", "us") and the customer named in the subscription ("Customer", "you") for the Caalano360 service (the "Service").

## 1. Roles

1.1 The Customer collects personal information about its leads, patients, clients and contacts through its own CRM, advertising and analytics accounts ("Customer Data"). The Customer determines why and how that information is collected and is the APP entity responsible for it under the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth). Where the *General Data Protection Regulation* (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") applies, the Customer is the controller.

1.2 Caalano Digital processes Customer Data only to provide the Service to the Customer and on the Customer's documented instructions. Where the GDPR applies, Caalano Digital is the processor. This DPA and the Customer's configuration of the Service are the Customer's complete instructions.

1.3 Information about the Customer's staff who hold Caalano360 logins (names, email addresses, sign-in and access records) is handled by Caalano Digital for its own account administration and security under its Privacy Policy, for which Caalano Digital is responsible.

## 3. Caalano Digital's obligations

Caalano Digital will:

3.1 process Customer Data only as set out in clause 1.2, and tell the Customer if it believes an instruction breaches the Privacy Act or the GDPR;

3.2 not use Customer Data for its own purposes, sell it, use it to train artificial intelligence models, or disclose it to anyone other than the sub-processors in clause 6 and the Customer's authorised users;

3.3 keep Customer Data separate from every other customer's data by row-level security enforced in the database, and give each authorised user access only to the accounts the Customer has assigned;

3.4 apply the security measures in Schedule 1 and not reduce them during the term;

3.5 ensure that its staff who can access Customer Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and have accepted the Caalano360 Terms of Use, and limit such access to what is needed to operate and support the Service;

3.6 assist the Customer, at no charge for reasonable requests, to respond to access, correction and deletion requests from individuals (APP 12 and 13; GDPR Articles 15 to 22), to conduct privacy impact assessments and to deal with the regulator;

3.7 pass to the Customer within 5 business days any request or complaint it receives directly from an individual about Customer Data, and not respond on the substance unless the Customer asks it to;

3.8 make available the information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and allow one audit per year by the Customer or an independent auditor on 30 days' notice, at the Customer's cost, conducted so as not to disrupt the Service or expose other customers' data. A current SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report from a sub-processor satisfies the audit for that sub-processor's scope.

## 4. The Customer's obligations

The Customer warrants that it:

4.1 has collected Customer Data lawfully, has given the notices and obtained any consents that the Privacy Act (APP 3 and APP 5), the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth), state health records laws and, where applicable, the GDPR require, including for health information and for call recording;

4.2 has the authority to connect each CRM, advertising and analytics account it connects, and to have Caalano Digital read from and write to it;

4.3 will assign logins only to people entitled to see the accounts assigned to them, and will remove logins promptly when that entitlement ends;

4.4 is responsible for the accuracy of Customer Data and for any instruction it gives through the Service, including replies sent and records changed through the CRM write-back features.

## 8. Data breaches

8.1 Caalano Digital will notify the Customer **within 48 hours** of becoming aware of any unauthorised access to, loss of, or disclosure of Customer Data ("Breach"), and will provide what it knows of the nature of the Breach, the data and individuals affected, the likely consequences and the steps taken.

8.2 Caalano Digital will assist the Customer to assess whether the Breach is an eligible data breach under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act and to notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals within the 30-day assessment window, and, where the GDPR applies, within 72 hours under Article 33.

8.3 Each party will bear its own costs of notification, except that Caalano Digital will meet the Customer's reasonable costs where the Breach was caused by Caalano Digital's breach of this DPA.

9.2 Within 30 days after the subscription ends, or earlier on the Customer's written request, Caalano Digital will delete all Customer Data, including credentials for connected accounts, and will confirm deletion in writing. Backup copies age out within a further [X] days. Caalano Digital may keep only what the law requires it to keep, and only for as long as the law requires.

9.3 A record deleted in the Customer's CRM is removed from the Service's copies at the next refresh, within 24 hours, without a request.

## Schedule 1: Security measures

- Transport encryption (TLS 1.2 or higher) for every connection.
- Credentials for connected accounts encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using per-record data keys wrapped by a master key held only in the hosting provider's secret store; master key rotation without re-encrypting data.
- Row-level security in the database, forced on every table that carries customer data, so that no query can read another customer's rows even by application error.
- Role-based access: Super Admin, Admin, User, Account Admin and Account User, with per-account assignment and per-tab visibility for customer-side roles.
- Password hashing with PBKDF2-SHA256 (150,000 iterations) and per-user salt; sign-in throttling; signed session tokens with per-user revocation.
- A sign-in gate at the network edge that withholds the application itself from anyone not signed in.
- An access log of screens, accounts and tabs opened by each user, retained 90 days and visible to Customer admins on request.
- Daily backups to a private repository with tested restore procedures; database point-in-time recovery [once the Neon plan that includes it is active].
- Automated checks and tests on every release, a staging environment, and a maintained security backlog reviewed each release.
- Production access limited to named staff using multi-factor authentication on the hosting, database, code and CRM developer accounts.
